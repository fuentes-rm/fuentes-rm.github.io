My teaching experience spans both graduate-level doctoral courses in robust and LMI-based control at the University of Talca and undergraduate engineering lectureships in control systems, linear dynamical systems, electromagnetism, and industrial automation at the Catholic University of Maule.

University of Talca — Faculty of Engineering

Curicó, Chile • Doctoral Program (Teaching Assistant, 2026)

Control Robusto de Sistemas Inciertos (Robust Control of Uncertain Systems)

Doctoral Course • Teaching Assistant

Modeling, analysis, and synthesis of robust controllers and filters for uncertain continuous-time, discrete-time, and impulsive hybrid systems. Covers polytopic and norm-bounded uncertainties, LTV/LPV gain-scheduled dynamic controllers, parameter-dependent Lyapunov functions, invariant sets, stochastic stability, and robust model predictive control (RMPC).

Uncertain & Hybrid Systems LTV / LPV Gain-Scheduling Parameter-Dependent Lyapunov MATLAB & YALMIP SeDuMi / MOSEK

Diseño de Sistemas de Control por Medio de Desigualdades Matriciales Lineales (Control Design via LMIs)

Doctoral Course • Teaching Assistant

Advanced formulation of stability and performance synthesis problems via Linear Matrix Inequalities (LMIs) and semidefinite programming. Covers Schur complement, Finsler's lemma, H₂ and H∞ state and dynamic output-feedback synthesis, polytopic/norm-bounded uncertainty robustness, and numerical conditioning using MATLAB and YALMIP.

Linear Matrix Inequalities (LMIs) Semidefinite Programming H₂ & H∞ Synthesis Dynamic Output Feedback MATLAB & YALMIP

Catholic University of Maule (UCM) — Faculty of Engineering

Talca, Chile • Undergraduate Courses (Lecturer in Charge, 2022–2025)

Sistemas de Control II (Computer-Based & Discrete-Time Control Systems)

Undergraduate • Lecturer in Charge

Sampled-data control systems, signal reconstruction, A/D and D/A conversion, Z-plane stability analysis, discrete state-space representations, pole-placement regulation and reference tracking, emulation of continuous controllers, and digital PID implementation.

Sampled-Data Control Z-Transform Pole Placement Digital PID

Sistemas de Control I (Automatic Control Systems I)

Undergraduate • Lecturer in Charge

Foundations of SISO feedback loops, classical PID design, frequency-domain limitations, internal model principle, feedforward and cascade control architectures, controller parameterization, and continuous-time state-space control design.

Classical & PID Control Internal Model Principle Cascade & Feedforward State-Space Design

Sistemas Dinámicos Lineales (Linear Dynamical Systems)

Undergraduate • Lecturer in Charge

Mathematical analysis of continuous and discrete-time linear systems using Fourier, Laplace, and Z transforms. Frequency response via Bode and Nyquist/Polar plots, linearization of nonlinear dynamics, and structural properties of stability, controllability, and observability.

Laplace & Z Transforms Bode & Nyquist Stability & Controllability Observability

Electrónica Industrial y Automatización (Industrial Electronics & Automation)

Undergraduate • Lecturer in Charge

Semiconductor power devices (diodes, transistors, thyristors), least-squares parameter identification, microcontroller/Arduino integration, Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) architecture and Ladder programming, industrial sensors/actuators, and industrial robotics kinematics.

Power Semiconductors PLCs & Ladder Logic System Identification Robotics Kinematics

Electromagnetismo (Electromagnetism)

Undergraduate • Lecturer in Charge

Electrostatics and magnetostatics (Coulomb, Gauss, Ampère, Biot-Savart, and Faraday laws), capacitance and inductance, RL/RLC transient and AC steady-state phasor analysis, transformers, and electromechanical energy conversion in electrical machines.

Maxwell & Faraday Laws RLC & Phasor Analysis Transformers & Electrical Machines
