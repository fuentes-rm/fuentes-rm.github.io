## Abstract

This paper investigates the problem of control design for dc–dc converters, suitable to address variations in the input voltage in photovoltaic systems and constant power loads. The proposed approach models the converters in terms of Linear Parameter-Varying (LPV) models to compute gain-scheduled robust controllers with H∞ disturbance attenuation and D-stability pole location constraints. The validation is made via simulation of classical buck, boost, and buck-boost converters.
