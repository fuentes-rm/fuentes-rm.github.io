## Abstract

This paper establishes novel synthesis conditions in terms of linear matrix inequalities (LMIs) for the design of a full-order dynamic output-feedback (DOF) controller for continuous-time Linear Parameter-Varying (LPV) systems. The proposed approach simultaneously minimizes the H2-guaranteed cost and ensures that the closed-loop eigenvalues (for a fixed operation point) stay within a predefined convex region of the complex plane. Additionally, the new conditions combine LMIs with fine-tuned scalar parameters to obtain less conservative results. The effectiveness of the control strategy is demonstrated through a Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) simulation of a DC-DC converter.
