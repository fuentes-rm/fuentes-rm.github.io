## Abstract

The present work tackles the H∞ packet-based control design in the autonomous vehicle platoon (AVP) problem based on digital redesign of a linear parameter-varying system under the presence of Denial of Service (DoS) attacks. Simulations are provided considering the time-invariant model of the AVP and the parameter-varying model with and without the presence of DoS attacks.
