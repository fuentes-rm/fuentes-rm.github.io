In this institution I did teaching in the following courses as a responsible professor:

Sistemas Dinámicos Lineales
======
[cite_start]This course focuses on the mathematical analysis of continuous and discrete linear systems. [cite_start]Students apply mathematical foundations—including Fourier series, Fourier transforms, Laplace transforms, and Z transforms—to analyze signals and dynamically represent systems. [cite_start]The curriculum explores graphical representation methods such as Bode and Polar/Nyquist diagrams, while introducing state variables, the linearization of continuous and discrete systems, and the core concepts of system stability, controllability, and observability.

Sistemas de Control I
======
[cite_start]This course explores the core foundations of automatic control systems. [cite_start]Students learn to analyze and design Single-Input Single-Output (SISO) control loops and classic PID controllers. [cite_start]The curriculum covers frequency domain design limitations, internal model principles, and the implementation of feedforward and cascade structures. [cite_start]Furthermore, the course delves into the parameterization of controllers, linear modeling in state space, and control design utilizing state variables.

Sistemas de Control II
======
[cite_start]Building upon Control Systems I, this advanced course introduces computer-based control systems, covering sampling, reconstruction, and analog-to-digital conversion. [cite_start]The curriculum emphasizes the analysis of discrete-time systems in the Z-plane, closed-loop stability, and state-space representations. [cite_start]Students design regulation and tracking loops via pole placement using polynomial and state-variable representations, and learn practical methods for approximating continuous controllers and implementing digital PID controllers.

Electromagnetismo
======
[cite_start]This theoretical-practical course introduces the foundational concepts of electricity and magnetism. [cite_start]Students learn to model and solve problems by applying fundamental laws, including Coulomb's, Gauss's, Ampere's, Biot-Savart, and Faraday's laws of induction. [cite_start]The comprehensive curriculum spans electric fields, potential, and capacitors, to magnetic fields, inductance, and RL/RLC circuits. [cite_start]It also covers notions of alternating current, including phasors, impedance, transformers, and electro-mechanical conversion in electrical machines.

Electrónica Industrial y Automatización
======
[cite_start]This course provides a comprehensive overview of the components, equipment, and automated systems utilized in modern industrial processes. [cite_start]Students gain conceptual frameworks and practical tools starting with semiconductor physics (diodes, transistors, thyristors) and digital logic circuits. [cite_start]The curriculum advances to parameter identification using the least squares method and Arduino integration. [cite_start]Key industrial topics include programmable logic controllers (PLCs) architecture and ladder programming, the application of resistive, capacitive, and inductive sensors, various industrial actuators, and an introduction to industrial robotics kinematics.
