In this institution I participated as a Teaching Assistant in the following doctoral courses:

Control Robusto de Sistemas Inciertos
======
This doctoral course introduces the modeling, analysis, and design of robust controllers and filters for uncertain continuous, discrete, and impulsive hybrid systems. The curriculum covers polytopic and norm-bounded uncertainties, time-varying systems (LTV/LPV) with scheduled gain for dynamic controllers, and suitable Lyapunov functions for each system class. Additional advanced topics include invariant sets in dynamics, stochastic stability, and robust model predictive control (RMPC). The course effectively bridges theoretical rigor with reproducible computational implementation using tools such as MATLAB, YALMIP, SeDuMi, and MOSEK.

Diseño de Sistemas de Control por Medio de Desigualdades Matriciales Lineales
======
This course provides advanced training in the analysis and design of controllers and filters using Linear Matrix Inequalities (LMIs), articulating rigorous theoretical foundations with computational practice in positive semidefinite programming. The syllabus covers the formalization of stability and performance problems in continuous and discrete time through Lyapunov functions and matrix equivalences, such as the Schur complement. It thoroughly explores H2 and H-infinity synthesis for state and output feedback, robust control against polytopic or norm-bounded uncertainties, and time-varying (LTV/LPV) systems. Students also learn practical guidelines for numerical conditioning, validation, and traceability of results using MATLAB and YALMIP.
