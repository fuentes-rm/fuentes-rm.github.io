In this institution I participated as a Teaching Assistant in the following doctoral courses:

Control Robusto de Sistemas Inciertos
======
[cite_start]This doctoral course introduces the modeling, analysis, and design of robust controllers and filters for uncertain continuous, discrete, and impulsive hybrid systems. [cite_start]The curriculum covers polytopic and norm-bounded uncertainties, time-varying systems (LTV/LPV) with scheduled gain for dynamic controllers, and suitable Lyapunov functions for each system class. [cite_start]Additional advanced topics include invariant sets in dynamics, stochastic stability, and robust model predictive control (RMPC). [cite_start]The course effectively bridges theoretical rigor with reproducible computational implementation using tools such as MATLAB, YALMIP, SeDuMi, and MOSEK.

Diseño de Sistemas de Control por Medio de Desigualdades Matriciales Lineales
======
[cite_start]This course provides advanced training in the analysis and design of controllers and filters using Linear Matrix Inequalities (LMIs), articulating rigorous theoretical foundations with computational practice in positive semidefinite programming. [cite_start]The syllabus covers the formalization of stability and performance problems in continuous and discrete time through Lyapunov functions and matrix equivalences, such as the Schur complement. [cite_start]It thoroughly explores H2 and H-infinity synthesis for state and output feedback, robust control against polytopic or norm-bounded uncertainties, and time-varying (LTV/LPV) systems. [cite_start]Students also learn practical guidelines for numerical conditioning, validation, and traceability of results using MATLAB and YALMIP.
